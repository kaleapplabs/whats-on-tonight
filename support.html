Getting started

  1. You need a Jellyfin server with some films or shows in it, reachable from your Apple TV (on your home network, or over the internet or a VPN).
  2. Open the app and choose Add server. Type the address, for example 192.168.1.20:8096 or https://jellyfin.example.com.
  3. Choose Add login and sign in with your Jellyfin user name and password.
  4. The app builds your channels. Choose Play now to open the guide.

Common questions

The app shows no channels.

The channels come from your library's genres, studios and people. A channel needs at least a few videos, set by Settings, Minimum channel size. Make sure your library has metadata, then choose Refresh from server.

A video won't play.

Choose Refresh from server. The app checks every file and lists any it cannot play under Settings, Skipped files, with the reason. Some formats are converted by your Jellyfin server, so the server needs enough power to do that.

The app can't find my server.

Check that the address is right and that your Apple TV can reach the server. Allow the app to find devices on your local network when it asks. A server on plain http:// is only allowed on a home network.

How do I lock the app?

Open Settings, Security and set a PIN for the login. The app asks for it when it opens and when you return after being away for a few seconds.

Can I hide or merge genres?

Yes, with optional settings stored on your own Jellyfin server. See below.

Optional server settings

By default every genre becomes a channel. To hide genres, merge two into one, or name genres in other languages, store a small block of settings in your Jellyfin user's display preferences, and every device you sign in on will use it.

The file is JSON with three optional parts:

  • hiddenGenres: genres that never become channels.
  • genreMerges: "name to fold": "name to fold it into".
  • genreNames: for each language code (es, fr, ja, de), "English name": "name to show".

A free loader script and full instructions are available on request through the contact email above. After loading, choose Refresh from server in the app.
