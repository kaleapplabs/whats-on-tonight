Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

The short version: What's On Tonight does not collect your data. It does not send anything to the developer or to any third party.

Who we are

What's On Tonight is made by k.a.l.e. App Labs. Contact details are on the support page.

What the app stores, on your device only

  • The addresses of the Jellyfin servers you add.
  • The names of the Jellyfin users you sign in as, and the sign-in token Jellyfin returns, kept in your device's Keychain. Your password is used once to sign in and is not stored.
  • An optional PIN lock, kept only as a one-way hash in the Keychain.
  • Your app preferences, such as theme, language, text size, hidden channels and channel numbers.

This information stays on your Apple TV. It is never sent to us.

What the app connects to

The app connects only to the Jellyfin server or servers you add, to read your library and play your videos. That server belongs to you or to whoever runs it for you, and its own privacy practices apply to the data on it. If you use a server run by someone else, they can see what you watch.

The app asks permission to find devices on your local network so it can reach a server in your home.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect personal information, usage data or device identifiers.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting services.
  • We do not sell or share data, because we do not have any.

Optional settings stored on your server

You can choose to store a small block of preferences (such as genres to hide) in your own Jellyfin user's settings. It lives on your server, and the app only reads it.

Children

The app has no accounts and collects no information from anyone, including children. What appears in the app is whatever your own server holds, so it is up to you to decide who may use it.

Apple

If you choose to share diagnostics with app developers in your Apple device settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash and usage reports. We do not receive anything else through Apple.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: see the support page.
