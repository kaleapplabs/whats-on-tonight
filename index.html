What's On Tonight

Your own Jellyfin library, as live-style TV channels on Apple TV.

Coming soon to the App Store

A real channel guide

Open the app and something is already playing. Flip channels, see what is on now and what is next, and watch any programme from the start.

Screenshots from the app, with sample films.

A channel for everything

Genres, studios and people become channels, with mixes such as Everything, Newest 20 and Clips.

Your library, your rules

The app is only a player for a server you already run. Nothing is uploaded and nothing is collected.

Several servers and logins

Switch between servers and people, with an optional PIN lock for each login.

Your language

English, Spanish, French, Japanese and German.

Pick your look

Modern, Retro, Paper and a Jellyfin theme, so the guide fits your room.

Everything in one place

Play now, Surprise me, browse every file, and see how big your library is, all from one screen.

You need a Jellyfin server

What's On Tonight contains no videos. It plays the ones on your own server.
