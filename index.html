What's On Tonight

Your own Jellyfin library, as live-style TV channels on Apple TV.

What's On Tonight turns the films and shows on your own Jellyfin server into a channel guide you can flip through, like cable TV. Open the app, pick a channel, and something is already playing, in the same place on every TV in the house.

What it does

  • A channel for everything. Channels are built from your library's genres, studios and people, plus mixes such as Everything, Newest 20 and Clips.
  • A real guide. A time grid with what is on now and what is next. Scroll through programmes, or watch one from the start.
  • Browse every file in your library, with rankings by newest, most played and largest.
  • Several servers and logins, with an optional PIN lock for each login.
  • Your language. English, Spanish, French, Japanese and German.
  • Looks you can change. Modern, Retro (several styles), Paper and Jellyfin themes.
You need a Jellyfin server. What's On Tonight is a player for a server you already run. It contains no videos of its own.

Privacy

The app collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics and no tracking. Read the privacy policy.
